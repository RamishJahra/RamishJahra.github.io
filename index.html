WORDS, STORIES & IDEAS

Some things
are better written.

Writing is a part of me that exists beyond technology. I write poetry, prose, and fiction, often drawing from emotions, people, memories, imagination, and the small moments that stay with me. Sometimes writing is simply a way of saying what is difficult to say aloud.

I don't write with a particular format or genre in mind. I write when I have something to express, whether it becomes a few lines of poetry, a piece of prose, or a story that grows in my head. It is one of the ways I slow down, observe, imagine, and understand the things around me. All I can say, if there wasn't writing, there would be a part of me that would feel incomplete.

My technical work and my writing may seem like different worlds, but both allow me to create something from an idea. One is built with code; the other is built with words.
